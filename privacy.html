This policy explains how Lagranthium ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and our internal tool, Lagranthium Uploader (the "Tool"), which uses YouTube API Services.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool or any content it publishes, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Who uses the Tool

The Tool is used solely by Lagranthium to manage YouTube channels that Lagranthium owns. It is not available to the public, and it does not connect to or collect data from any other person's Google or YouTube account.

3. Information the Tool accesses

  • Our own channel and video data: channel identifiers, and the titles, descriptions, tags, visibility settings, upload status and basic public statistics (such as views and likes) of videos on our own channels.
  • Authorization tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google for our own accounts, so that the Tool can act on our channels.

The Tool does not access, collect or store personal information about YouTube viewers, commenters or subscribers.

4. How information is used

We use the information above only to upload our own videos, set their metadata and visibility, and keep an internal log of what has been published. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

To help write video titles and descriptions, the Tool sends the video's file name, series name and our own draft description text to an AI text service (Anthropic's Claude API). No YouTube user data is included.

5. Storage and retention

Authorization tokens are stored in encrypted form on a computer controlled by Lagranthium. Our publishing log (video identifiers, titles and dates) is stored in a private Google Sheet owned by Lagranthium. We keep YouTube API data only as long as needed to operate our channels, refresh any stored statistics at least every 30 days, and delete it when it is no longer needed or when authorization is revoked.

6. Revoking access

Access granted to the Tool can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page of the connected account. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens and stop accessing the related data.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics. The Tool does not place cookies on anyone's device.

8. Children

Our content and the Tool are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
