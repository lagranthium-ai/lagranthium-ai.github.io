Lagranthium Uploader

Lagranthium Uploader is an internal publishing tool used only by Lagranthium to manage its own YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos we produce, set their titles, descriptions, tags and visibility, and read basic statistics for our own videos. It is not offered to the public, and no third-party user accounts are connected to it.

Our videos are made with the help of AI tools for narration and visuals, and are labelled as such on YouTube.
